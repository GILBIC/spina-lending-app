# Reporting evidence and statement package

This implementation adds direct cash flows, changes in equity, preceding-period comparatives, retained Management notes and reviewed cash/noncash journal components to the posted-ledger statement pack. Migration0161 adds financing-liability reconciliation and explicit coverage of every posted journal. Every section uses the same database snapshot and book identity. It does not assert that all business activity has been recorded or that an annual statutory package has been approved.

## Cash definition and classifications

Management retains a versioned cash definition for an open fiscal period. The current supported controls are 1010 office cash, 1020 Collector custody and 1030 bank/wallet cash. The policy pins account identities, codes, system keys, types, normal balances and posting roles. Every report and new classification checks that definition again. A changed definition makes cash totals unavailable until a new reviewed policy is retained; it cannot turn existing cash into a complete zero report. Actual inclusion, basis and notes are explicitly reviewed. Other investments or restricted balances are not automatically treated as cash equivalents. A policy change preserves earlier versions and requires new classifications under the new policy.

Each posted cash line is allocated in exact signed cents among operating, investing, financing and internal amounts. Splits must equal the original line and preserve its direction. Internal allocations must offset within one journal or the exact protected principal legs of one owned transfer in the selected period. The repository derives transfer grouping from current exact0153 posting audits and their mapped cash lines; user classifications cannot supply a group identity. Two different transfers, corrected proof, fee amounts, duplicate legs, unselected cash accounts and legs outside the period cannot qualify as a pair. An unmatched transit leg cannot disappear as an internal transfer. Fees remain separately classified. Opening-balance cash entries and their protected reversals are displayed as opening adjustments and cannot be labelled operating receipts or financing proceeds by this API.

The report shows gross receipts and gross payments in each category. Any missing or invalid classification makes the complete net cash-flow amount unavailable. A zero net amount does not hide two unclassified opposing cash movements. Classified net flows plus opening cash and separately disclosed opening adjustments must equal selected GL cash balances at period end.

Protected Collector settlement cash uses the same principle. When both custody and receiving cash controls are selected, the exact remitted principal is an internal handover, not a second business receipt/payment. Actual excess remains separately classified external cash. The repository derives this split from current exact 0155 evidence; corrected or missing proof cannot restore gross business classifications. Actual Collector returns retain their own external cash classification. If a custody account is outside the selected definition, an absent cash counterpart cannot be offset. Grouping authority comes only from the repository; exported exact proof identifiers describe the calculation and cannot be submitted as grouping authority.

The design follows the distinction between operating, investing and financing cash flows, separate noncash disclosure and reconciliation to financial position described in the [IFRS Foundation's IAS7 overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-7-statement-of-cash-flows/). Actual framework applicability and individual classifications remain retained entity-specific facts; the user has authorized the software design.

With 0160 credit-funded remittances, only gross custody less approved credit is the physical internal handover. Actual excess is separate. The credit discharge has its own accepted-date journal (2220 to 1020), while actual cash retains the count date. A zero-cash settlement creates no receiving-wallet cash event or internal handover pair. Under a retained definition including1020, the approved discharge is a payment from cash already held in custody. It requires current exact application proof, a negative1020 line, zero internal principal and an explicit external cash classification. It is neither another receipt nor a hidden internal offset. If1020 is excluded, the liability/custody component is noncash under that selected definition. This interpretation follows the distinction between cash on hand and internal cash transfers in [IAS7](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-7-statement-of-cash-flows/); no additional Treasury payment is fabricated.

## Mixed journals and financing liabilities

Management retains a financing-account policy for the period, including an explicit empty selection when none apply. Each reviewed journal is decomposed into positive exact-cent portions linking one original debit and one original credit. Portions must cover every line exactly. Cash portions must agree with the current retained cash-flow classifications. Noncash investing, financing, combined investing/financing, operating and other reclassifications remain separate; no grand noncash total is inferred. A1,000 asset financed with400 cash and600 borrowing discloses600 once.

Every posted journal has a current review, a protected opening/close exclusion, or a visible blocker. Missing reviews, unknown/corrected source proof, changed policies, changed account semantics and changed cash classifications prevent a complete coverage result. Each selected financing liability reconciles opening balance, cash changes, noncash changes and protected opening adjustments to its closing GL balance. Incomplete coverage leaves those movement totals unavailable. The main statements and comparatives read this evidence in the same snapshot as their balances.

Both clients guide account/amount/evidence entry, show unallocated portions, and require review before saving. Lost responses retain the original request for unchanged retry. Current private grants for every contributing wallet, including opening balances and both Collector wallets, are checked before any figures are returned. Evidence retention shares the protected period-transition boundary. Retained reviews enter the close manifest; new versions require an open period. An accounting close does not itself certify disclosure coverage. Complete the presentation reviews before moving to period review; post-close presentation amendments are not implemented.

## Equity and comparatives

Opening and closing equity include unclosed earnings. The rollforward adds the selected period's profit or loss and other posted equity movements, excluding the formal period-close transfer. This keeps total equity unchanged when temporary accounts are transferred to retained earnings. A difference is shown explicitly.

The comparative is the immediately preceding fiscal period by end date, with its actual dates displayed. It is not silently presented as a prior year or an equal-length period. Its statements, cash definition, notes and classifications are read in the same snapshot as the selected period. No previous period produces a null comparative.

## Retained evidence and boundaries

- Policies and cash classifications are immutable versions with current Management authority, exact retry semantics, book binding and atomic audit writes. They create no GL entry or money movement.
- Notes preserve supplied title, text, reference and digest. They are labelled Management evidence; the system never manufactures an accountant's approval.
- A policy or classification can be corrected through a new version while the period is open. Historical exact retry returns the original result, with current authorization checked again.
- The older noncash inventory remains a compatibility fallback before0161. Its gross debit is a journal reference amount, not an additive financial-statement total. With0161 the clients show the complete candidate/review coverage and financing reconciliation instead. Unsupported source adapters remain blockers, even when their journals balance.
- Source completeness and annual statutory-package completeness remain separate assurance flags. Missing source adapters and dated assessments cannot be cured by adding a report note.

Validation includes exact classifications, immutable evidence, book/permission checks, loss/profit/zero-activity periods, formal close and comparative reconciliation. The execution ledger records the currently completed runs and remaining integration work.


## Full-range review download

The Web General Journal export and Android Financial Statements download accept complete contiguous configured periods covering up to 366 inclusive days. The optional reporting package preserves the existing journal, ledger, trial balance and audit files and adds exact JSON, period coverage, and a printable statement copy. One authorized repeatable-read, read-only snapshot supplies every balance, comparative, cash classification and disclosure review. A denied historical private-wallet grant blocks the entire download before monetary aggregates.

The selected-range statement sums nonclosing income, expenses and equity movements in decimal cents, uses the first opening equity and the last financial position, and displays reconciliation differences. Cash flows are recomputed from actual cash lines. Matching repository-proven owned-transfer principal legs inside the range offset internally even when they cross monthly boundaries; explicit fee classification remains. A different, changed or missing cash definition makes range cash totals unavailable. Financing-liability movements use the full range only when the retained financing definitions agree; unresolved period reviews remain explicit blockers. Notes retain their original period identities.

A prior-year range is included only if the same calendar dates (February 29 maps to February 28) are covered by complete configured periods. Dates and basis are included in the JSON. Missing prior books leave the comparative null. Individual period statements retain their separately labelled immediately preceding-period comparisons. No company, balance, accounting framework, taxpayer identity or classification is inferred. The package supports accountant review and does not certify statutory acceptance or comprehensive source recognition.

Migration0170 makes existing protected capital, legacy disbursement/collection/remittance and authentic reversal, renewal-boundary EIR, initial and later ECL, and V1 tax postings eligible for disclosure review only when their retained audit and actual journal agree. Unknown source types and altered evidence remain blocked. Historical display-name changes and retirement do not invalidate the new capital/ECL predicates; changes to required account semantics do. Evidence review still requires current authority and an open period.

Renewal completion remains in the existing protected payout source family. Its current posting proof must include the retained treatment, measurement and accounting-asset lineage; disclosure retention locks those sources with the existing payout evidence. Web and Android Payout Accounting provide a separate renewal-treatment review before the usual preparation and posting confirmations. The reviewer must upload a private bundle containing the signed renewal contract and settlement/measurement support, explicitly attest that it matches the displayed schedule and references, choose modification or derecognition, and supply the applicable policy and measurement evidence. The clients preserve old/new loan dimensions and separate gain/loss components and reject cash accounts in the completion journal. The UI does not choose an accounting treatment or infer fair value.

For a credit-impaired predecessor, the renewal review displays the retained allowance before the boundary, signed gross/net allowance reconciliation, signed impairment adjustment and allowance after the boundary. The last amount must equal the allowance transferred by a modification. Reconciliation and impairment adjustment retain their separate balanced journal pairs on the old operational loan; a negative reconciliation reverses the exact interest-revenue/allowance pair. The supported policy blocks derecognition until successor initial credit status and credit-adjusted EIR evidence can be handled; the screen displays that blocker and leaves the treatment choice blank. Modification carries the existing impairment basis forward and does not establish a cure.

## Dated assessment review navigation

The historical loan assessment screen in each client opens the selected fiscal period and loan. It offers an explicit retained credit-risk review selection for the server-authorized Stage 1 reviewed 12-month or Stage 2/3 lifetime policy. Exact payoff and renewal settlement instead have their own zero-exposure rationale reviews; the old renewed operational loan does not reuse the successor's assessment. Preparation and journal confirmation remain separate actions.

An actor with `accounting.ecl.credit_risk_label.review` can open the current-cutoff label review from the same screen. The server supplies the actual permissible cutoff, schedule, DPD and unpaid amount. All financial labels and rebuttal choices begin blank. The reviewer supplies the existing retained-label evidence fields, including separate rebuttal/write-off/recovery support when applicable. This action retains a risk review and creates no financial journal. It cannot create a review for a past cutoff by changing the device date.

If a modified loan has no review of its own, the screen identifies the inherited credit-risk review and predecessor loan separately. This can retain Stage 1, Stage 2 or Stage 3 history. It shows the retained stage, default, write-off support and recovery labels as historical context. They do not prefill the new review. Stage 3 impairment still requires current supporting evidence and an explicit review to cure; Stage 1/2 history does not establish impaired net-interest measurement.

A period with zero temporary income/expense balances can complete the protected close without a closing journal. This includes an empty or capital-only first period when the server's source checks permit closing. Both clients still require the retained preparation, exact zero net income, close digest, explicit confirmation and final close audit. Periods with nonzero temporary balances require the actual closing journal even if their net income is zero. The screen identifies when no closing journal is required and displays the retained post-close earnings balance.

A blocked credit-impaired candidate lists the exact actual cash dates that need measurement evidence. Each action opens that cash application's verified after-cash carrying amount and its opening impairment assessment or retained renewal review. These opening references remain distinct; a same-day successor cash application can use a verified renewal opening, while a fiscal opening assessment must precede the cash date. The reviewer enters dated receipt scenarios; the endpoint does not accept an allowance target, interest rate, stage or invented impairment date. This retains measurement evidence only. The fiscal assessment later recognizes the aggregate net-interest adjustment and the remaining impairment adjustment once.

The clients distinguish whole-period gross interest, whole-period net interest revenue, unposted gross interest tail, signed gross/net allowance reconciliation, impairment P&L adjustment and total allowance balance change. Source-authoritative gross interest can differ by a cent from directly rounded net interest; the reconciliation preserves that signed difference without clamping either interest amount. An elapsed negative reconciliation requires the retained interest currency adjustment. When supplied, subtracting this adjustment must leave nonnegative independently rounded gross interest and economic unwind. Older positive-reconciliation payloads can omit it. For gross-carrying-less-expected-PV measurements the clients preserve the raw weighted present value, verify final currency rounding with integer decimal arithmetic and display the separate ECL rounding adjustment alongside the rounded PV and ECL. A half-cent loss cannot disappear because the PV display was rounded first. These controls implement the selected source contract; they do not select the entity's accounting framework or provide missing dated evidence.
