# First accounting book

The owner has no existing accounting books. Setup begins with the available business evidence and does not require an old ledger, a second business or invented opening balances.

1. Verify whose transactions the book records, retain the identity evidence and configure that identity once. Choose the correct actual entity kind. Book identity is permanent; selecting it does not post money.
2. Configure the real fiscal dates and the reporting basis. Pin the deployment to the resulting book identifier. Configure authorized users, wallets and cash mappings for this same entity.
3. Review whether cash, outstanding loans, obligations or other balances already exist at the start date. Use available contracts, receipts, wallet records and other retained evidence. No old books does not mean no balances. Leave unsupported amounts pending rather than fill them with zero or a balancing plug.
4. Where opening balances are required, verify the protected workbook and its treatment of prior activity before preparing and posting the exact opening journal. If no opening balances are required, skip the workbook and record actual transactions directly. Record actual capital contributions through protected initial funding; do not post the same money again as both opening cash and capital funding.
5. Prepare and confirm supported source accounting, review unpaid liabilities and corrections, then reconcile the posted ledger against the underlying source records. A cash receipt and its later application represent one cash movement.
6. Review financial statements, cash classifications, equity movements and disclosure evidence. The first book may have no comparative period. The reporting package retains this absence explicitly and does not create previous-year figures. Multi-period packages retain each period and the whole-range ledger for accountant review.
7. Close only after current source evidence, reconciliations and the retained close review agree. An empty first period needs no synthetic zero journal. New or changed source activity invalidates the affected review; concurrent source activity returns a retry. Keep the database and private evidence together in backup and restore verification.

This runbook describes the software workflow. Actual identity, dates, evidence and amounts must come from the business. A successful synthetic test does not establish those facts or activate a production book.
