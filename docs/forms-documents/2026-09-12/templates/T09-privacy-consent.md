I. PURPOSE OF THIS CONSENT

This Form records the Borrower's consent and authorization for SPINA LENDING COMPANY to collect, record, organize, store, use, update, retrieve and otherwise process personal information in connection with the Borrower's loan application and, if approved, the resulting loan and related transactions, subject to applicable Philippine data privacy laws and regulations.

This Form should be read together with SPINA LENDING COMPANY's applicable Privacy Notice. Where processing is required or otherwise permitted by law, the applicable lawful basis shall govern even where separate consent is not required.

II. PERSONAL INFORMATION THAT MAY BE COLLECTED AND PROCESSED

Depending on the transaction and applicable requirements, SPINA LENDING COMPANY may process information provided by the Borrower or obtained from lawful sources, including:

• Basic identifying information, such as name, date of birth, civil status, citizenship and contact details;

• Residential and mailing address and other contact information;

• Government-issued identification information and identification documents;

• Controlled identity-verification evidence references used in the CIF process, including evidence relating to eGov verification of National ID and TIN ID and, where applicable, live-face/selfie and liveness verification evidence;

• Employment, business, professional and income information;

• Loan application information, requested loan amount, purpose, payment frequency and loan term;

• Credit, payment, financial and existing-obligation information relevant to credit evaluation and loan servicing;

• Information contained in supporting documents and communications relating to the loan;

• Information necessary for account administration, payment processing, collection, dispute handling, fraud prevention, compliance and recordkeeping; and

• Other information reasonably necessary for a lawful purpose connected with the Borrower's transaction with SPINA LENDING COMPANY.

III. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

I understand and authorize SPINA LENDING COMPANY, to the extent permitted by law, to process my personal information for:

• Receiving, evaluating and processing the loan application;

• Verifying my identity and information submitted in the application;

• Conducting credit investigation, background verification and related risk assessment;

• Contacting references, employers, businesses or other lawful sources identified by me for verification purposes;

• Preparing and administering the Loan Disclosure Statement, Loan Agreement, Promissory Note, payment schedule and other loan documents;

• Releasing and disbursing loan proceeds and recording payments and transactions;

• Maintaining my account, statement of account and payment history;

• Communicating with me regarding the application, loan, payments, notices and account matters;

• Managing delinquency, default, lawful collection activities, restructuring, settlement and related account administration;

• Preventing, detecting and addressing fraud and unauthorized transactions;

• Complying with applicable laws, regulations, regulatory requirements, court orders and lawful government requests; and

• Performing other legitimate and lawful functions reasonably necessary to administer the lending relationship.

IV. CREDIT AND INFORMATION VERIFICATION AUTHORIZATION

I expressly authorize SPINA LENDING COMPANY, subject to applicable law, to verify the information provided in my application and supporting documents. This may include contacting or obtaining information from my stated references, employer, business, or other lawful sources for purposes connected with evaluation, administration and servicing of my loan.

I understand that inaccurate, incomplete or misleading information may affect the evaluation of the application and may be addressed in accordance with the Loan Agreement and applicable law.

V. DISCLOSURE OR SHARING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

I understand that SPINA LENDING COMPANY may disclose or share relevant personal information where reasonably necessary for a lawful and stated purpose, including with authorized employees and representatives, service providers or contractors performing functions for SPINA LENDING COMPANY, payment or disbursement providers, professional advisers, auditors, insurers or other applicable parties, regulators and government authorities, and persons to whom disclosure is required or permitted by law.

SPINA LENDING COMPANY shall limit disclosure to information reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose and shall apply appropriate safeguards required by applicable law.

VI. ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS

I consent, where applicable and subject to law, to receive legitimate account-related communications through the contact details I provide, including notices concerning the loan application, documents, payment schedules, reminders, statements, account status, default, settlement and other servicing matters.

I understand that electronic communications do not remove SPINA LENDING COMPANY's obligation to comply with applicable requirements concerning notices, disclosures and data privacy.

VII. DATA RETENTION AND SECURITY

I understand that personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, administer the lending relationship, comply with legal and regulatory obligations, establish or defend legal claims, and for legitimate recordkeeping purposes, subject to applicable retention periods.

I understand that SPINA LENDING COMPANY is expected to implement reasonable organizational, physical and technical measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss or other unlawful processing.

VIII. RIGHTS OF THE DATA SUBJECT

Subject to applicable law and its limitations, I understand that I may have rights as a data subject, including the right to be informed about processing, access personal information, correct inaccurate or outdated information, and exercise other rights available under applicable data privacy laws and regulations.

Requests concerning personal information or applicable data subject rights may be directed to SPINA LENDING COMPANY through its designated Data Privacy Officer or privacy contact identified in its current Privacy Notice.

IX. CONSENT AND ACKNOWLEDGMENT

I acknowledge that I have read and understood this Form and the applicable Privacy Notice made available to me.

I voluntarily provide the consent and authorizations stated herein for the purposes described above, to the extent that consent is the applicable lawful basis for processing.

I understand that withdrawal of consent, where legally permitted, does not necessarily affect processing already undertaken on the basis of prior valid consent and does not prevent SPINA LENDING COMPANY from continuing processing where another lawful basis exists or where processing is required or permitted by law.

I understand that refusal to provide information necessary for a lawful loan evaluation or transaction may prevent SPINA LENDING COMPANY from processing or completing the relevant application or transaction.

X. OPTIONAL / SEPARATE CONSENTS

The following consent is optional, is not required to obtain a loan, and applies only if I separately select it and the processing is legally permissible:

☐ I consent to processing of my personal information for lawful service-related communications beyond those necessary to administer the loan, subject to SPINA LENDING COMPANY's Privacy Notice.

Any additional optional processing must be specifically described to me in writing before I am asked to consent. Optional consent is separate from processing necessary for the loan or supported by another lawful basis.
