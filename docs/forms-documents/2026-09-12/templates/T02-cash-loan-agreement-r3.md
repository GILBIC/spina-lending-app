5. COMPLETE CONTRACTUAL SCHEDULE OF PAYMENTS

Before actual cash release, SPINA shall generate the complete contractual Schedule of Payments from the exact locked approved terms. The BORROWER shall be given the opportunity to review and sign/acknowledge the complete schedule together with the final loan packet.

If the intended release date changes before cash handoff and the dates in the contractual schedule would become inaccurate, SPINA shall regenerate the affected schedule from the same approved material terms and obtain the required borrower acknowledgment/signature before release.

Annex A and all system-generated continuation pages form part of this Agreement and share the same document/packet version.

6. CURRENT OPERATIONAL SCHEDULE

After release and posting of transactions, SPINA may maintain a current operational schedule reflecting actual payment performance. The operational schedule may change succeeding payment dates, remaining scheduled collection dates/installments, outstanding balance, and the Current Operational Finish, as permitted by the signed documents and applicable law.

An operational schedule update does not silently amend the signed principal, contractual interest method or basis, agreed installment amount, payment frequency, disclosed charges, Contractual/Original Maturity, or any other material contractual term. The signed contractual schedule remains the historical contractual reference.

7. APPLICATION AND POSTING OF PAYMENTS

Payment allocation is system-controlled. Collector or Staff users shall not manually choose or override the contractual allocation path.

a. For a Regular Cash Loan: oldest Past Due scheduled obligations first; then obligations Due Today; then any true excess only through the authorized Advance or Principal Reduction path.

b. For a 7x7 Cash Loan: oldest Past Due Interest; Today’s Interest; oldest Past Due Principal; Today’s Principal; then Advance, subject to the locked Disclosure and Schedule.

c. For a 7x7 Cash Loan, Extra Principal is separate from Advance and may be applied only after all current and past-due interest is fully satisfied. When allowed, Extra Principal keeps the agreed daily/installment amount and may shorten the remaining tail of the schedule.

d. Any true unapplied excess that cannot lawfully be applied to a surviving obligation shall be handled through the disclosed Refund Due process and shall not be silently netted.

Physical cash collected by the LENDER or an authorized Collector is recognized at the actual physical receipt event and is then posted to the authoritative account record. A third-party payment screenshot or unverified transfer is not official proof of receipt until settlement or receipt is verified through the applicable authorized channel.

8. BORROWER ACCOUNT AND VIEW-ONLY ACCESS

Where SPINA provides a borrower portal, website or mobile application, it serves as a view/verification tool for the BORROWER’s account records. It may display the original loan terms, payment history, current amount due, outstanding balance, current operational schedule, lawful charges, and finalized documents.

Online access does not replace any notice, disclosure or delivery method that applicable law or the final loan documents require through another channel.

9. ACCOUNT REVIEW AND ERROR REPORTING

The BORROWER should review account information made available by SPINA and promptly report any suspected posting, balance, payment or schedule error. SPINA shall review available supporting information and correct a verified error through the controlled record-correction process.

10. LATE PAYMENT, DELINQUENCY AND DEFAULT

A missed or late payment may place the account in a past-due or delinquent state. It does not automatically create a new legal acceleration event unless the applicable counsel-approved contractual/legal criteria are met. Any late/default charge must be the exact lawful amount or rate stated in the Loan Disclosure Statement; if the Disclosure states None / PHP 0.00, no such charge applies.

If a legally recognized event of default occurs, the LENDER may exercise only those remedies available under the final loan documents and applicable law, including lawful demand and collection proceedings.

11. ACCELERATION

Where acceleration is legally and contractually permitted, the LENDER may declare the amount legally collectible immediately due, subject to any required notice or other applicable condition. Acceleration does not authorize collection of future unearned interest, undisclosed charges, or amounts not otherwise legally due.

12. COLLECTION COSTS AND ATTORNEY’S FEES

V1 does not impose an automatic flat collection-cost or attorney’s-fee amount. Any such amount, if ever sought, must be actual, documented, lawful, case-specific, properly provided for in the applicable loan documents, and subject to Management/legal review. Collection activity shall comply with applicable Philippine law and fair collection standards.

13. VOLUNTARY PREPAYMENT AND PAYOFF

The BORROWER may voluntarily prepay the Loan in accordance with the product rules and applicable law. Advance Payment, Extra Principal/Principal Reduction, and Full Payoff are distinct transaction types and shall not be silently treated as the same event.

For full payoff, the system-calculated amount consists of remaining principal, earned and unpaid interest, and any other exact lawful amount then due. For 7x7, contractual interest included in the payoff cannot extend beyond the signed Contractual Maturity or earlier payoff, as applicable. Future unearned interest is excluded. The prepayment charge must match the exact locked Disclosure; where it states None / zero, no prepayment charge applies.

14. ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS

Subject to applicable law and the BORROWER’s registered contact details, the LENDER may send legitimate loan-related communications through authorized channels such as email, SMS, borrower portal/app notification, courier or other lawful means.

SPINA shall distinguish a document or notice that was merely Generated from one that was Sent, Delivered/Confirmed, or Failed, where the selected communication provider makes such status available.

15. ELECTRONIC RECORDS AND AUDIT EVIDENCE

Electronic records maintained by SPINA—including authoritative account entries, approvals, document versions, payment events, notices, system timestamps, actor identities and other audit evidence—may be used as records of the Loan transaction, subject to applicable law and rules on electronic evidence.

16. DATA PRIVACY

Personal information relating to this Loan shall be processed in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, applicable National Privacy Commission issuances, and SPINA LENDING COMPANY’s then-current Privacy Notice.

The Privacy Notice is the detailed privacy document for the categories of personal data processed, purposes and lawful bases, recipients/processors, retention, safeguards, data-subject rights, privacy contact/DPO information and, where applicable, controlled identity/CIF evidence such as eGov National ID/TIN evidence references and live-face/selfie/liveness evidence.

The separate Data Privacy Consent and Authorization Form records consent or authorization where consent is the applicable lawful basis. Neither the Privacy Notice nor the Privacy Consent Form constitutes loan approval, a loan contract, or proof that Loan proceeds were released.

20. SEVERABILITY

21. MATERIAL AMENDMENTS AND VERSIONING

A change to a material signed term—including principal, contractual interest basis/method, payment frequency, agreed installment amount, or a disclosed charge—requires the approved amendment/versioning process, appropriate borrower acknowledgment/signature, and regeneration of the remaining contractual schedule as required.

Where a remaining schedule must be regenerated after a material amendment, the new schedule begins from the first completely untouched future installment; past or already-touched rows remain preserved under the prior version.
