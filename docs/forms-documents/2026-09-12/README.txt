ASSEMBLY BOUNDARIES
The contractual packet is the Agreement + Disclosure + Promissory Note + complete
Schedule of Payments. At assembly, expand/replace the abbreviated Agreement Annex
with the complete same-version Annex A; do not issue two competing schedules.
The two-page standalone Annex is a reusable layout, NOT a complete real borrower
schedule, fixed installment count, or page limit. All approved rows are required.
Optional notarial components are not mandatory for every loan. Their applicability
and actual final page count must be resolved for the assembled instrument.
Application/CIF acknowledgment, privacy acknowledgment, loan-contract signing,
actual office cash receipt, and notice transmission remain separate events.
Deliberate wet-signature lines are valid in a signing-ready copy; do not fabricate
signatures or actual receipt events to fill them. The signed evidence copy must
capture the real required acts. No unresolved financial values in issued output.

SCHEDULED REMAINING PRINCIPAL — APPROVED MEANING
The principal expected to remain after that contractual installment, assuming all
scheduled payments are made fully and on time. It excludes interest, fees and
penalties and is not the borrower's live account balance or payoff amount.
Use the same locked principal components. The server-side projection/binding and
its tests are still implementation work, not implemented by this bundle.
