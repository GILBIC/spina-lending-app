# Official requirements and unresolved applicability

Research basis: **20 September 2026**. This dated source summary supports engineering preparation. The owner and accountant must confirm the current issuances and RDO/LT-office requirements for the actual taxpayer before filing.

## Initial system registration

RMO 9-2021 distinguishes CAS, CBA and components. Determine the actual system category, included branches and maintenance arrangement; the name “Spina” does not select a category. Registration acknowledgement is not blanket software certification. Preserve a reviewed system version and assess later changes for the applicable update/notification route. [RMO 9-2021, sections III–V](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMO%20No.%209-2021.pdf). The earlier PTU requirement was removed by [RMC 5-2021](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMC%20No.%205-2021.pdf).

The 2026 Charter lists counter and ORUS application routes. Its standard documents include the applicable Annex C/E sworn statement, Annex C-1 system summary, applicable output/books samples, an audit-trail printout and signed Annex B technical checklist. Representative authority and parent/affiliate licensing evidence depend on the arrangement. The ORUS entry specifies JPEG, PNG or PDF attachments with a 25 MB maximum. The engineering ZIP is not one of those attachment formats. [2026 Citizen’s Charter, printed pages 66–68 / PDF pages 75–77](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen%27s%20Charter%20%282026%20Edition%29v2.pdf).

**Form-source gate:** the separately applicable current RMO 9 Annex B/C-1 form bytes have not been verified in this package. Obtain current official forms and reconcile the entire checklist before signing. The retrieved historical [RMC 5-2021 Annex B](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMC%20No.%205-2021%20Annex%20B.pdf) is labelled as that source only. It is not a retrieved RMO 9 Annex B, and its older retention/invoice-expiry wording must not override later issuances. A preparation field list is not an official form.

## Annual books registration

Computerized books ordinarily require annual registration within 30 days after taxable-year close or cessation, subject to the applicable rule or valid extension. [RMC 3-2023](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMC%20No.%203-2023.pdf).

RMC 4-2026 requires computerized and permanently bound loose-leaf books registration through ORUS. Manual handling is exceptional: retain the official outage advisory or actual error screenshot and follow the concerned office's process. ORUS generates the authentic QR stamp; keep the computerized-books QR for records. The February 17, 2026 computerized-books extension was specific, not a recurring deadline. Loose-leaf invoices/receipts and other records retain their separate process. [RMC 4-2026, pages 1–2](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%204-2026.pdf).

## Retention and document validity

RR 7-2024 section 4 provides a general five-year accounting-record preservation basis, measured from the day after the return deadline or actual late filing date for the year of last entry. Computerized records are retained electronically. Material records for unresolved protests/refund or tax-credit claims remain until final resolution; longer applicable legal duties must also be considered. This does not authorize automatic deletion at five years. Keep an approved retention/hold policy and backup coverage. [RR 7-2024, section 4](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf).

The former five-year receipts/invoices expiry must not be added to new output as a general requirement. [RR 6-2022](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%206-2022.pdf), [RMC 123-2022](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMC%20No.%20123-2022%20%281%29.pdf).

## Invoice, SAF and electronic reporting decisions

Invoices are the principal sales document under the EOPT changes; an ordinary collection receipt or downloaded record copy is not automatically the appropriate tax invoice. VAT/non-VAT issuance treatment requires actual taxpayer and transaction review. This package makes no determination about the taxation of loan principal, interest or charges. [RMC 77-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2077-2024.pdf).

A PDF is not an auditable electronic SAF merely because it can be downloaded. Spina's CSV books export needs a separate applicable SAF specification and assessment; no validated SAF capability is asserted. [RMO 9-2021](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMO%20No.%209-2021.pdf).

Electronic invoices require structured, extractable data; a printed or PDF rendition alone does not establish that capability. [Official RR 11-2025 digest](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%2011-2025.pdf). RR 26-2025 sets December 31, 2026 for its listed covered invoice-issuance groups. Its e-commerce micro-taxpayer exemption is not a blanket exemption from every independent category. Other coverage and electronic-sales reporting depend on the stated BIR-system/separate-regulation conditions. Record electronic invoicing and sales reporting as separate applicability decisions, both pending until supported. [RR 26-2025, pages 1–2](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%2026-2025.pdf).
