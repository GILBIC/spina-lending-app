# Retained period review manifest

Migration 0151 pins the source and ledger identities accepted when an authorized Management actor moves a period from open to review. It adds immutable review versions and binds a close preparation to one version. The existing source gates remain prerequisites; a manifest cannot waive a blocker or certify comprehensive coverage.

## Canonical snapshot

The cutoff is the fiscal period end in Asia/Manila. Retain deterministic JSON sorted by table, immutable identity and line ordinal. Sources include relevant earlier facts needed to support carried balances, not merely transactions inside the period. Do not include live fiscal status, timestamps updated by the review transition, user access grants, or unrelated future transactions.

| Family | Retained source identities and fields | Cutoff treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Book and period | Configured `book_identity` row; period ID, label, start/end; manifest policy and supported family list | No invented identity for a previously unconfigured legacy book; retain the explicit absence. Configuration changes the digest. Period workflow status is excluded. |
| GL and chart | Every posted journal through cutoff, all retained lines with account/client/loan dimensions; chart identity, code, name, type, normal balance and posting/active/system flags for those lines | Exclude only this period's protected `period_close:<period UUID>` close entry so its authorized creation/posting does not stale its own preclose source proof. Prior periods' closing entries remain opening-balance evidence. |
| Treasury cash | Original events, effective movement lines, event revisions, source links and attached evidence identities/digests; original openings | Event/line business date through cutoff. Revisions of an old original remain historical-source changes. Opening original facts are retained; later supersession status/version is not a new money event. |
| Treasury receipt | Original receipt ID/event/account/context/client/amount/effective date; dated application and refund legs, retained receipt journal preparation/posting | Exclude mutable `applied_amount`, `refunded_amount`, `version`. Derive cutoff application totals from actual correctly funded collection dates and cutoff void evidence; derive refunds from `receipt_refund` source links and their actual event dates. |
| Treasury application | Application original coordinates, amount and source-result identities; actual linked collection evidence and its protected preparation/posting | Include actual collection dates through cutoff; malformed/missing links use Manila recording-date fallback. Current reversal state is replaced by dated void/reversal evidence. A future application cannot alter an earlier receipt snapshot merely by changing receipt capacity. |
| Treasury transfer/payout | Original transfer amount/accounts/context, source leg event through cutoff, destination leg only when its actual event is through cutoff; loan payout source and immutable posting audit | Exclude transfer's mutable version and any not-yet-effective destination leg. Preserve valid 1040 transit at the earlier cutoff. Coordinate with 0150 audit names rather than inferring a second leg from a parent status. |
| Collector | Original opening/case/credit recognition facts and dated immutable entries, resolutions, settlements, actions and their source evidence | Retain original recognition amounts and linked IDs. Exclude current capacity/status/embedded history arrays on mutable case and credit aggregates; dated entries supply changes. Zero-effect PASS settlements remain auditable facts even though they are not accounting blockers. |
| Lending | Collections, dated voids, releases, cancellations, renewals and custody observations, received remittances and transfer evidence | Normalize future void/cancellation state out of earlier originals. Retain contract coordinates and actual custody legs through cutoff. Exclude later operational balances/loan statuses and mutable update timestamps. |
| Measurement | Contract schedules/registrations/installments, relevant 7x7 policy/anchor evidence, ECL label/forecast/quantitative evidence and protected allowance/writeoff/recovery audit; exact 0148 assessment preparation/confirmation | Retain the inputs referenced by dated loan proofs and measurements, plus their applicable supersession/revocation evidence. Do not hash every future review of the same loan. Unsupported historical states remain blockers under the existing assessment policy. |
| Tax | Dated immutable DST, percentage, return, payment, adjustment, amendment, refund and credit evidence; referenced rule evidence; preparation/posting/line proofs | Actual recognition/payment/adjustment/refund/application dates through cutoff. A future payment of a valid payable cannot alter the old outstanding amount. |
| Liabilities | Recognitions and dated line claims through cutoff, their source journals, retained account mapping and evidence coordinates | Derive outstanding amount from the dated claims. A future settlement does not change an earlier manifest; an unregistered or changed old line remains a source blocker. |

Protected preparation/posting evidence is selected by its exact relevant source or journal identity. Historical originals and immutable audit fields are retained in full where possible; mutable parents get explicit field projections and dated child legs. Private payloads never cross the API boundary. A public summary may expose only manifest ID/version, digest, counts, current/stale/missing state and `comprehensive_source_coverage: false` under existing accounting authorization.

## Lifecycle and atomicity

Wrap the controlled status function without rewriting its legacy close logic. Require and lock the current active Management role and `accounting.period.manage`, serialize the period with its existing advisory key, take the fiscal row lock and the established source lock order, and require current source gates clear. Compute and retain a new immutable manifest immediately before the authorized open-to-review transition. Any failure rolls the capture and transition back together. A direct transition that bypasses capture is rejected by a table guard.

Return-to-open remains allowed only under the existing rule: no immutable close preparation exists. A later review creates a new manifest version even if its economic digest is identical. Prior manifests remain immutable. The latest review version is bound to a new close preparation in a separate immutable binding row.

Prepare and post wrappers require current Management permissions, the same locks, and exact current snapshot equality. Table guards independently check preparation admission and transition to closed; deferred guards recheck before commit, including source changes made later within the same transaction. Exclude the protected closing entry from the preclose snapshot, while the original close engine continues to verify its exact coordinates and temporary-account zero balances.

Use READ COMMITTED for mutations and run a fresh volatile snapshot query after locks. Hold all source and proof relation locks to commit, including the manifestation audit insert locks. Reporting can compare a retained snapshot with current sources without mutation locks in one repeatable-read transaction. Late actual-money records remain admissible after a review/close commits; they make the live manifest stale and restore source warnings. Never alter the old manifest to absorb them.

Existing review or closed periods without a retained manifest must remain explicitly unretained. Do not fabricate historical review evidence during migration. New review captures are server-derived under the existing user action; there is no checkbox acknowledgement or additional external approval gate.

## First RED cases

Retain a real reviewed receipt and assert immutable snapshot identity; require new versions after open/review; reject a changed historical chart at preparation even when source counts and balances are unchanged; retain and expose stale late source evidence; permit a later dated receipt application and refund without changing the earlier snapshot; exclude only the protected closing entry; enforce direct/deferred admission and current role; test a writer winning on either side of the lock. Parent executes the disposable PostgreSQL window serially. This design is a field-level contract and does not assert that implementation or tests have passed.
